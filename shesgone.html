She's gone

I went out to my garden to move her, I had created a cute little home out of clay and I was going to place her in a forest nearby my property. However, when I went out into my garden, I began to hear her sing again and felt very sleepy. Everything went black and wehn I awoke, all the flowers on the bush were gone. I looked around my garden looking for her when I noticed that most of my plants have been devoured. It looks as if millions of caterpillars have ravaged their way through my plants.

I can't understand why she left. I didn't want her to leave me, I just wanted to preserve my garden. I hope she comes to visit sometime.
