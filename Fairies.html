My Fantastical Discovery

I am simply dumbfounded at what I have discovered. I recieved a package yesterday of a shrub with instrucions on how to plant it. The senders name and address were worn out. I could only make out this persons initials, which are F.A. Whoever you are, you have the same initials as my husband :) The instructions do not identify the species or where it origionates from, however, I didn't want to disapoint the sender who put in the effort to send this to me so I found a nice spot in my garden and planted it. Miraculously, it grew into a fully developed bush in about a day. Beautiful flowers also sprouted all over it, they had a similar appearaence to the Campanula, but were bigger and grew seperatly as apposed to in clusters. When I went to examine one of them, I noticed a small creature curled inside. I took a closer look and realized it was a FAIRY! I gasped and spooked it. We locked eyes for a moment and it gave me a sweet smile and proceeded to touch my nose with its tiny hand. What do I do with this creature? I can hardly even believe that this fanciful creature exisits let alone how to care for it. It has been eating some flowers from my other bushes which I would rather it not but how could I deny such a beautiful and sweet creature from simply taking what it needs.
