Eleanor's Gardening Club

My name is Eleanor Alnwick, Dutchess of Northumberland. My children have suggested that I create a website on the internet so as to share my passion with the world. I suspect that this is their way of getting their bothersome old mother to rant about her boring hobbies with those on the internet instead of them. I was reluctatnt at first, but I came to appreciate it as I can now communicate with likeminded individuals who enjoy my ramblings :)

I have always had a green thumb since I was a young lass. My father used to tell me that it wasn't lady like for a girl to be playing around in the dirt like a mutt, that I wouldn't find a husband that way. His silly opinion unfortunatly swayed me away from my dreams of being a gardener then. Now as an older women, with the acres of land my father had left me after his death, I get to live out this dream. With my newfound time, I have nurtured an extensive and lucious garden. My hopes for this website are to share what I have learned with others and showcase the fruits of my labour.
